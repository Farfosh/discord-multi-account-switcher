    How it works

    This does not copy the account open in your current tab. Create a profile, then sign in inside the separate Discord window. Chrome keeps that login in the isolated profile; no tokens are copied.

    Names, tags, and pictures are local labels. Changing them never touches the Discord account or its session. Never paste a Discord token anywhere.
